Your Daily Habits ✔

💧 I carried and used a reusable water bottle today instead of buying single-use plastic.

🚲 I chose to walk or bike to get around rather than travelling by car.

💡 I turned off the lights and switched off devices when I left the room.

♻️ I sorted my rubbish correctly and recycled everything that could be recycled.

🛍️ I refused single-use plastic bags and packaging throughout my day.

🌱 I composted my food scraps instead of sending them to landfill.

Why These Habits Matter for Aotearoa

💧 Water New Zealanders use about 200 litres of water per person per day. Cutting your shower by just 2 minutes saves up to 20 litres.

♻️ Recycling NZ sends over 3 million tonnes of waste to landfill every year. Correct recycling helps divert materials back into use.

🔌 Energy Standby power can account for up to 10% of a home's electricity bill. Unplugging unused devices adds up quickly.
