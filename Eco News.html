Predator Free 2050: NZ reaches milestone of 1 million hectares protected

New Zealand's ambitious Predator Free 2050 programme has reached a landmark milestone, with over one million hectares now protected from introduced predators such as stoats, rats, and possums. Kiwi populations in protected areas have increased by 25% over the past five years.

NZ on track to reach 90% renewable electricity by 2027

Contact Energy and Meridian Energy's latest wind and geothermal projects have pushed New Zealand's renewable electricity generation to 87%, with analysts expecting the government's 90% target to be met ahead of schedule.

Auckland beaches pass water quality tests after wetland restoration

Three Auckland beaches previously closed to swimming have reopened after a community-led wetland restoration project dramatically reduced stormwater contamination. The project planted over 40,000 native plants along the Tāmaki Estuary.

New soft plastic recycling drop-off points open across NZ schools

The Soft Plastics Recycling Scheme has expanded to include collection points in 450 schools nationwide. Students can now bring soft plastics from home to be processed into new products, diverting an estimated 200 tonnes of plastic from landfill annually.

Taranaki iwi leads NZ's first community carbon sequestration forest

A Taranaki iwi has established New Zealand's first community-owned carbon sequestration forest under a new indigenous land management framework. The 3,000-hectare project will sequester an estimated 120,000 tonnes of CO₂ over 30 years while restoring habitat for rare birds.

Wellington's electric bus fleet now one of the greenest in the Southern Hemisphere

Wellington has completed the transition of its entire urban bus fleet to electric vehicles, making it one of the greenest public transport networks in the Southern Hemisphere. The move is expected to reduce transport emissions in the region by 18,000 tonnes of CO₂ per year.

NZ schools reduce food waste by 30% through new composting programme

A Ministry of Education composting initiative has helped 300 schools reduce cafeteria food waste by an average of 30% over 12 months. The programme provides compost bins, training, and curriculum resources to help students understand the food waste cycle.

Waikato River health improves as farming runoff rules tighten

Water quality monitoring across the Waikato River shows a measurable improvement in nitrate and sediment levels following the introduction of stricter farm runoff regulations in 2024. Māori fishers report seeing more native species returning to previously degraded stretches.
